  description: >-
    Out of Range is a geometric composition built from intense colour, simple signs and scattered
    structures resembling fragments of a technical interface. The contrast between cool grey, bright
    green, yellow and black creates a space somewhere between a map, a device screen and an abstract
    landscape. Individual dots and signs look like information detached from its original context.
    The work leaves open the question of the boundary between what we can see and record and what
    remains beyond our reach.
