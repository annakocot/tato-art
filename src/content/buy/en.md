---
title: How to buy an artwork
introduction: >-
  Every purchase begins with a conversation. These simple steps help you choose an artwork and
  arrange the details directly with the artist.
steps:
  - title: Choose your artwork
    description: >-
      Explore the gallery and choose a work that speaks to you. Note its title or artwork ID. If you
      have questions about size, technique or availability, I am happy to help.
  - title: Contact me and arrange payment
    description: >-
      Write to me with the title or artwork ID. We will confirm availability, the price, payment
      method and delivery details before completing your purchase.
  - title: Arrange packaging and delivery
    description: >-
      We will discuss protective packaging, shipping costs and timing individually. Let me know your
      delivery location so I can provide the details.
  - title: Ask about the certificate
    description: >-
      I will confirm the Certificate of Authenticity details for your chosen work during our
      conversation. You can also ask about the signature, edition and care instructions.
closing: Have a work in mind, or need a little help choosing? Get in touch.
