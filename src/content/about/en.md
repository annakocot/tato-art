**Art Factory Lines is an independent art project focused on original, physical works of art.**

Each work emerges from an individual creative process shaped by years of experience in graphic design, underground culture, independent publishing, music and visual experimentation.

The works combine painting, graphic composition, typography and raw forms with influences from minimalism, Bauhaus, modernism, underground culture and independent music scenes. Each piece is a unique artwork rather than part of mass production.

All works presented in the gallery are physical originals. Each comes with a **Certificate of Authenticity** and is carefully prepared for worldwide shipping.

**Thank you for supporting independent art and human creativity.**
